About Sumeru Principles and boundaries

Licensing

Two editions, one codebase.

The framework and the standard business addons are open source under Apache 2.0 and always will be — the enterprise edition adds addons and services on top of that same core.

Community

Available

Apache 2.0

Free forever. Self-hosted. No user limit, no feature gate, no phone-home.

  • The whole framework — ORM, module system, UI engine, RBAC
  • All standard business addons
  • JSON-RPC API and the addon scaffolder
  • Multi-company on one database
  • Commercial use, modification and redistribution
  • Community support
Start here

Enterprise

Planned

Commercial licence

Everything in Community, plus the addons and services that pay for the open-source work.

  • Everything in the Community edition
  • Enterprise addons — advanced reporting, approvals, deeper localisation
  • Studio: models and views without writing Go
  • Supported upgrade path between versions
  • SLA-backed support and implementation help
  • Indemnification for regulated deployments
How this works
Not for sale yet. Sumeru is pre-alpha and the enterprise edition does not exist as a product today — no addons, no contract, no price list.
01

The core stays open

Nothing that is open today will be moved behind the commercial licence.

02

You are never locked in

Stop paying and your data, schema and own addons keep working on the community edition.

03

Your addons are yours

Apache 2.0 imposes no copyleft on what you build against it.
