    App launcher

    Quickly jump to any menu or app without browsing sidebars.

    Open the launcher

    Press Cmd+K (Mac) or Ctrl+K (Windows/Linux), or click the search icon in the top bar.

    What it searches

    The launcher matches app and menu names from your installed modules — not record content. Type a few letters (e.g. “pipeline”, “invoice”) and select a result to open that screen.

    Not AI search

    This is keyword matching on menu labels. Natural language search (e.g. “show me open leads from last week”) is planned but not available in the core product today. The optional Sumeru AI module adds placeholder hooks only.

    Keyboard shortcut on Apps and Settings

    On the Apps and Settings pages, press / to focus the page search filter quickly.
